## Genuine parts catalogues (OEM part numbers)

Use these to confirm a Mitsubishi part number against the car's VIN or chassis code (V98W).

* [Amayama — Mitsubishi Pajero catalogue](https://www.amayama.com/en/catalogs/mitsubishi/pajero) — genuine EPC, sells and ships from Japan.
* [epc-data — Mitsubishi Pajero](https://mitsubishi.epc-data.com/pajero/) — free diagrams and part numbers.
* [Megazip — Pajero V98W (2008–2009)](https://www.megazip.net/zapchasti-dlya-avtomobilej/mitsubishi/pajero-montero-4375/v98w-10699/lyuj1-625273) — diagrams and ordering.
* [PartSouq — Mitsubishi Pajero](https://partsouq.com/en/catalog/genuine/vehicle?c=Mitsubishi) — genuine catalogue and prices.
* [Nengun — Pajero V98W genuine parts](https://www.nengun.com/oem/mitsubishi/pajero-v98w) — genuine parts from Japan.
