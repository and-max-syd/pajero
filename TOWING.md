# Towing

How to move the car from where it sits to Ben's driveway. It is a short local job in the St Ives / Roseville area. Do not drive the car — the radiator is holed and the engine has no cooling.

See the plan: [Stage 2 — Get the car home](PLAN.md).

## What to ask for

* A flatbed (tilt-tray) tow for a short local move.
* Ask for a flat rate, not a per-kilometre rate.
* Tell them the car rolls, steers, and brakes. That makes loading easy and can lower the price.
* Rough expectation: **A$100 to A$180** for a short local tow. Ring two or three for quotes.
