# Replacement parts

Parts to find, buy, and fit. Buy second-hand where safe. Buy new for anything that keeps the engine cool or the car safe. Confirm each part against the car before you buy it.

Prices are rough estimates in Australian dollars. They are a guide, not a quote. Get the real price before you commit.

Status words: **Needed**, **Found**, **Bought**, **Fitted**.

## Cooling

Keep these new or known-good. They keep the engine alive.
